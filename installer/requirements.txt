USDprep - USD scenes made ready for Nuke

Turns heavy production USD scenes into light assets that Nuke reads:
pick an object in the 3D view or the scene tree, press Export.

What this computer needs

  - Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  - A graphics card with a current driver from its vendor (NVIDIA, AMD or
    Intel) that provides OpenGL 4.5. Any computer that runs Nuke 16 or 17
    qualifies. Without it the 3D view stays empty; the usdcut command line
    tool works regardless.
  - About 100 MB of disk space

What this installer does

  - Installs for the current user only. No administrator rights, no
    internet connection, nothing outside the chosen folder except the
    Start menu shortcut (and, if you tick it, an entry in your own PATH).
  - Everything the program needs is inside its folder. It does not touch
    Nuke, Python or any other USD installation on this computer.

Exports are .usdc files with a textures folder next to them - the form
Nuke 16 and 17 were measured to read, textures included.
